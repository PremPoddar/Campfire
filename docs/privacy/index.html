Privacy policy

Campfire and your data

Last updated 22 October 2026

In short: Campfire runs entirely on the host’s phone, keeps nothing after a Campfire ends, and has no accounts or servers. Chat on the local network is not encrypted.

What Campfire collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or servers of ours.

While a Campfire is lit

Messages, rooms and files live on the host’s phone, in memory and in the app’s cache, so everyone connected can see them.

When it ends

Every message, room and file is deleted. The cache is also cleared whenever a Campfire starts.

Not encrypted

Browsers only allow encryption on pages with a trusted certificate, which a local network can’t have without warnings. So someone watching the same Wi-Fi could read messages and room passwords, and direct messages pass through the host’s phone. Don’t share anything you wouldn’t say out loud in the room.

Link previews

The host’s phone fetches titles and pictures for links people post. It refuses any address on the local network, so a link can’t be used to look at your router or other devices.

Children

Campfire isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes
