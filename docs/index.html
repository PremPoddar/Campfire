Under the hood

How it works

01

Start a Campfire

Your phone becomes a tiny web server on your Wi-Fi or hotspot, and shows a link and a QR code.

02

Friends join

They open it in any browser, pick a name, and they’re in — no app, no account.

03

Put it out

End the Campfire and every message, room and file is wiped from the phone.

Privacy

Nothing left behind — and honest about the rest.

Campfire stores nothing after it ends and talks to no server. It isn’t encrypted on the local network, because browsers can’t trust a certificate there; the app says so plainly.

Read the privacy policy →

  • No accounts, no internet needed
  • Everything wiped when the Campfire ends
  • Link previews can’t probe your local network
  • Plain about what isn’t encrypted
